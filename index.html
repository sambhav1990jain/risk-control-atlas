How this database is built

A structured library of risks and controls, organised so that the same underlying risk can be read through two different professional lenses. Selecting an industry gives you everything that industry's audit or ICFR function needs \u2014 the processes specific to the sector alongside the finance, people, technology and governance processes every organisation runs. Selecting a lens changes what you are shown about each risk. The last option in the industry list opens the entire database at once.

The two lenses

  • Internal audit — risk, audit objective, what to review, field procedures, red flags, evidence to request, and the measures that indicate whether the process is working. Scope is operational, compliance and strategic, not only what touches the financial statements.
  • ICFR — the risk–control matrix: control objective, control description, preventive or detective, nature, frequency, financial-statement assertions, COSO component, key-control and fraud flags, segregation-of-duties considerations, and the design and operating-effectiveness test steps with population and sample guidance.

Assertion codes

E/O Existence or occurrence
C Completeness
A/V Accuracy and valuation
R&O Rights and obligations
CO Cut-off
P&D Presentation and disclosure

Frameworks referenced

  • COSO Internal Control — Integrated Framework (2013), five components
  • IIA Global Internal Audit Standards (2024)
  • IFRS 15 / ASC 606 for revenue-recognition risks
  • Sector regulation where relevant — hours of service, dangerous goods, customs

Status

Version 0.2. Twenty-eight industries are listed; every one loads the cross-industry foundation, and sector-specific content is layered on top as it is written. Populated so far: cross-industry, transportation and logistics, banking, insurance, manufacturing, retail and aviation. Content is written from practice, not copied from any proprietary source.
