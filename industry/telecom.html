Telecommunications

Telecoms handle enormous transaction volumes that pass through several systems before becoming a bill. Records lost along the way are services given away free.

What usually goes wrong here

  • Usage recorded on the network that never reaches billing, so it is never charged
  • Services activated for customers who were never properly credit assessed or set up to be billed
  • Amounts owed by other operators settled using their figures rather than your own
  • Network changes made without control, where an outage becomes a reportable event
  • Long-lived free-of-charge, test and employee accounts with real consumption

Compliance areas to check locally

Expect obligations covering the operating licence, service continuity and outage reporting, subscriber identity and data retention, consumer contract and billing transparency rules, and interconnect settlement terms. The specific law, regulator and reporting standard differ by country.
