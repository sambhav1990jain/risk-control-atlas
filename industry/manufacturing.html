Manufacturing and industrial products

A manufacturer buys materials, converts them, and holds significant stock throughout. Most of the value — and most of the risk — sits in that stock and in the accuracy of what it cost to make.

What usually goes wrong here

  • Costs used to value products that no longer reflect what inputs actually cost, so margins are wrong everywhere
  • Material that goes in but does not come out as sellable product, absorbed into variances instead of investigated
  • Stock that exists on the system but not on the floor, and counts performed by the people responsible for it
  • Product that will never sell at full price still carried at full cost
  • Safety and environmental conditions that must be evidenced, not merely asserted

Compliance areas to check locally

Expect obligations covering workplace safety, environmental permits and emissions reporting, product safety and liability, waste handling, and inventory valuation standards. The specific law, regulator and reporting standard differ by country.
