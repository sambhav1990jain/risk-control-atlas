FMCG and consumer goods

Consumer goods companies sell through retailers and distributors and spend heavily on promotions to hold shelf space. That spend, and product safety, carry most of the risk.

What usually goes wrong here

  • Promotional money committed without anyone checking whether the promotion worked
  • Deductions taken by customers against invoices and settled without being verified
  • Stock pushed into the distribution channel near period end to meet targets, returning later
  • Batch traceability that has never been tested, so a recall would be slow
  • Short-dated stock still valued as though it will sell at full price

Compliance areas to check locally

Expect obligations covering food and product safety systems, labelling and advertising claims, recall and withdrawal procedures, competition rules on pricing and distribution, and trade promotion accounting. The specific law, regulator and reporting standard differ by country.
