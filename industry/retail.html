Retail chains

Retail is a very large number of small transactions across many locations. No single one matters, which is exactly why losses accumulate without anyone noticing.

What usually goes wrong here

  • Refunds, voids and discounts processed against real sales, with the cash taken
  • Stock lost to theft, process error and short deliveries, all blended into one shrinkage figure
  • Money promised by suppliers counted as profit before the conditions to earn it have been met
  • Gift cards and loyalty points, which are money owed to customers and easy to understate
  • Store-level performance measured on sales, with control quietly deprioritised

Compliance areas to check locally

Expect obligations covering consumer protection and pricing accuracy, card payment security, product and food safety where relevant, employment standards, and stored-value or unclaimed balance rules. The specific law, regulator and reporting standard differ by country.
